While my own life has undergone drastic reinvention by matters both outside of and within my control, more and more people have gained class consciousness and begun finding one another to build communities of care. 

By now it’s incredibly clear to me that the myriad “unprecedented, historical” events caused by Capitalism that people have seen happen between May 2023 - January 2025 has raised the collective consciousness. It is my sincere belief that RAW foresaw the breaking point correctly, whether or not the math is correct. Thanks in part to people in the “Western” world refusing to back down from taking action against Empire because of the funding of “Israel” and keeping the plight of the people of Palestine in mind, we have reached or even surpassed the “51%” of people with raised consciousness needed to create a drastic change in society. Even without taking the opinions and ideas of a single human being as truth, this planet has reached its breaking point.

The way i’ve come to understand my self, my reality, and my future through the Eight Circuits of Consciousness has given me the tools needed to navigate through this volatile, ever changing world. After 38 years i’ve figured out how i can calm myself down, come into my body, experience the full range of my emotions and feelings, and have built a spiritual understanding on top of a system few people remember or know about. i’ve added, subtracted, reconfigured, and manipulated the Eight Circuits to meet my needs. It’s finally time to let you in on something: the entire intention of this work is to show you that you can do the exact same thing for yourself. What i intend to have done with this book is to have let you in on my understanding in the hope that you might be inspired to build your own.

Let this be a lesson to you, person who may have misinterpreted the intent of this work: no authority will ever save you from yourself. You may allow others to force your mind into a new way of being for your own reasons but you are succumbing to the will of another if you do. You are more powerful than you realize. Though life may sometimes have moments of despair, failure, and rejection it also has the ability to be joyous, hopeful, and inspiring. To succumb to despair is to allow the systems that have already failed you, and will continue to fail you, to win.

My life has already changed dramatically. From May 2023 i have gone from being a trans woman just beginning to question her gender expression to a transexual without gender. i’ve gone from being in a mindset of monogamous romantic relationship to polyamorous relationship anarchy, from single to having two partners with plenty of other people to play with. From stuck in my parent’s home to living in an apartment with a roommate. From broke to slightly less broke. Miserable to happy. Scared to brave. In search of my purpose to engaged with it daily. All of this is because of a single event that broke my mind and let me build a new foundation. i found the tools myself, put them to work, and built a life i am proud of and love deeply. 

For some, the way i’ve built a new life may seem ridiculous. Building a system of understanding through media consumption is not the average path taken, certainly. The difference is mindful media consumption. i do not numb out at the end of the day with a glowing rectangle in my face as a rule. i engage with consensus reality as little as possible, focusing strictly on the way the world is changing and the political implications thereof. Long gone are the days where i would binge watch a show, play a video game for 10 hours, or engage with trite celebrity gossip. Whenever i engage with entertainment, it’s for the value i hope to find in it. i don’t always find that value, but when i do it can be life altering. i limit my exposure to modern electronics as much as i possibly can so that when i do use them, they feel like one small aspect of my life instead of being my whole life, which was the way they used to feel for me. Because of this mindfulness, because i’ve decided to protect myself from the glowing and ever present screens in modern “Western” society, i have grown significantly.

As someone who has intersections between a few of these interconnected classifications (and make no mistake that people are classified in these ways to separate them from what Empire wants to make or assert is “normative”) i have experienced discrimination but nowhere near as much as some have.
