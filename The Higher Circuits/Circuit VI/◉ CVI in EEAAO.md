Back in the IRS Building, Evelynt and Waymondt are reconciling in front of the Alpha Jumpers who are being led by αGong-Gong. Jobu Tupaki comes back into frame, some of the Alpha Jumpers behind her. Jobu pulls Evelynt back into the Temple ‘verse one last time to tempt her mother and Evelynt immediately jumps out. Jobu scoffs, realizing that Evelynt and Waymondt are mending their broken relationship. Jobu tells Evelynt that even though she’s feeling good now, eventually that good feeling will go away. In an effort to finally destroy everything Evelynt knows of this ‘verse, Jobu grabs her mother’s hand and uses it to make another vaginal hand gesture. The power Jobu puts into this brings the Everything Bagel onto the upper level of the IRS above the atrium where everyone is standing. Evelynt is aghast at the sudden storm of wind and noise that Jobu has unleashed. A few people on the same level as the Bagel get sucked into it (including Daniel Kwan!). Jobu yells at her mother that she doesn’t care if Evelynt comes with her and to enjoy her life. Jobu has resigned herself to the idea that even though her mother understands her now better than ever, Evelynt finally has something she wants to protect (her marriage) and isn’t willing to give that up.

Evelynt immediately takes action and cuts off Jobu’s ascent up the staircase that will lead directly to the bagel. Jobu smirks and moves out of the way of the staircase, allowing Deirdre and the Alpha Jumpers to attack. Evelynt begins to use violence and attacks the Alphas back using her now-trained ability to verse jump. As she does, Waymondt calls out to her: “Evelyn, please!” Waymond doesn’t want her to fight anymore. Evelynt listens and lets herself be captured by a small crew of Alpha Jumpers. Jobu steps over her mother who is now being held down on the stairs. Evelynt  calls out to Joy to tell her to stop what she’s doing. Somewhere away from the action, Deirdre pulls a blade from an office paper cutter, then returns to Evelynt where she swings it at full force towards Evelynt’s neck. 

Back in the Taxes ‘Verse, Evelyn approaches Deirdre who is sitting in a chair outside of the laundromat. It has only been a few moments since Evelyn was let go, and Deirdre is visibly upset, trying to deal with what Waymond told to her inside. Evelyn asks what Waymond said. It turns out that Deirdre was married once herself as she explains her ex-husband served her divorce papers then she drove his car through their neighbors’ kitchen in a fit of blind rage. Deirdre recites a well-rehearsed motto to Evelyn, saying, “It’s cold, unloveable bitches like us that make the world go ‘round.”

	We ‘verse jump with Evelyn in this moment to the Hot Dog Fingers ‘verse where her romantic relationship with Hot Dog Fingers Deirdre is about to end. Jumping further back still, Evelyn finds moments where the two of them are showing one another love and care. Deirdre plays “Clair de Lune” with her feet after Evelyn asks Deirdre to play something for her. In the IRS ‘verse, the blow from the paper cutter blade is stopped with effort from Evelynt who is channeling the power of her feet skills from the Hot Dog Fingers ‘verse. Evelynt begins to realize that the power her feet have in the Hot Dog ‘verse aren’t the only power that ‘verse contains. It also gives Evelynt much needed insight into who Deirdre is as a person. In a universe where the two adversaries love each other, it brings Deirdre’s vulnerabilities and weaknesses into focus. Instead of using these aspects against the lonely, misunderstood IRS agent, Evelynt instead insists that no matter what, Deirdre is loveable:worthy of love. This attack on her mentality is a shock and the grip on the paper cutter blade becomes visibly weaker. It allows Evelynt a chance to fight back in a whole new way: with love. Suddenly, IRS Deirdre is pulled at the collar by one of Evelynt’s feet and into her arms. Multiple ‘verses flash between them where Evelyn is showing Deirdre love. Deirdre is reduced to tears. She falls back and sits at the foot of the stairs, weeping quietly that now she can feel.

  Just as Evelynt realizes this Jobu ascends the stairs and the Alpha Jumpers take over the staircase. αGong-Gong shouts out for the Jumpers to open fire. Deirdre reaches out her hand to plead for them to stop but it’s too late. Bullets go flying from every available firearm directly at Evelynt. She steps in front of Deirdre to protect the IRS agent and closes her eyes tightly. Round after round is fired from the Alphas. As Evelynt opens her eyes because she hasn’t been shot, she looks up to her forehead and sees a bullet directly above and between her eyes. She grabs it and pulls it away, and as the camera pans back from her face we see all of the other bullets that would have hit her are stuck against her body in the same way the one on her forehead was. A brief jump to the Taxes ‘verse shows that version of Evelyn pick up the baseball bat she used against the machines and window of the laundromat. As that Evelyn turns that bat over in her hands, she sees that it has a pair of google eyes stuck to it. Evelynt giggles slightly at the idea that the bullet in her hand could instead be a google eye. Evelynt pulls that image out of the Taxes ‘verse and into her own, where she sticks the now-google eye onto her forehead where the “third eye” would be. We quickly learn through visual cues alone that something has shifted dramatically in Evelynt’s understanding of life. Her “third eye” is open. She’s learned so much that she’s had an Awakening of her own.

  With her newly Awakened mind, we see a visual representation of the Circuit V trap that RAW describes about people who have recently moved into CV consciousness: “The first lesson the fifth-circuit adept has to learn is to control this upsurge of altruism and not make a nuisance of oneself by trying to force everybody to be happy…” When Evelynt causes the other bullets-cum-google eyes away from her body, that is simultaneously showing the outward effects of an Awakened mind on people around the Awakened person as well as Evelynt’s intent to help the people around her with her sudden knowledge and burst of outward loving energy. This also indicated that Evelynt is learning an entirely new skill: Kindness Kung-Fu. She even tells Waymondt after he asks what she’s doing, “I’m learning to fight like you.” 

One by one Evelynt makes her way through the Alpha Jumpers by giving them the gift of marriage, blasts of perfume, sexual gratification, and much more. Those first four Alpha Jumpers were handled using Circuits II, III, and IV respectively. The couple that Evelynt marries is an expression of emotional and territorial connection (CII). The perfume blast she gives to the older man who patronizes the laundromat plays on CIII by putting his mind into the past where he can reminisce strongly about his wife who passed away. The sexual gratification of the office worker is CIV, where Evelynt is finally able to reconcile that different kinds of sexual play are just as valid as others by practicing domination against the worker in two ‘verse’s simultaneously. Evelynt takes the office worker’s belt from him after he collapses and turns it into a whip, pulling Jobu down the stairs where she takes her final form as Chaos Jobu.

At the halfway point of the staircase, a large flat area leads to a turn towards the upper set of stairs. Here rests another Alpha Jumper we immediately recognize as the Racoccoonie Chef. The chef and Evelyn, in the Raccaccoonie ‘verse, are sitting outside of the restaurant where they worked. She had just gotten the chef fired. He’s aimless, feels like he has no skills of his own, and doesn’t know what he’s going to do with his life now that his best friend, and the real chef, is gone. He even says he’s “...useless alone.” But this ‘verse’s Evelyn reassures him, “We’re all useless alone.” Back in the IRS building, Evelynt has grabbed the Alpha Jumper version of the chef by his hair and tells him, “It’s a good thing you’re not alone.” In both ‘verses, Evelyn mounts his shoulders. In the Restaurant ‘verse, she says they’re going to rescue Raccaccoonie and in the IRS building she starts directing him to fight the other Alpha Jumpers that still stand in her way. This reinforces just what Chef Evelyn said: even when were lonely we’re never alone. It’s very easy to read Evelynt resting on his shoulders and directing him as a metaphor for the advice and wisdom we get from our loved ones that can sometimes direct our actions. Every kernel we’ve heard from our caregivers, friends, family, lovers, and more still sits deeply within our minds just waiting for a chance to be known again with as much power as they held when we were once told them.
	
With the remaining Alpha Jumpers now defeated:placated with different objects that meet their needs, we see Chaos Jobu staring into the void that is the Everything Bagel. By now we realize that Evelynt has completely mastered ‘Verse Jumping. Throughout this entire fight, using Kindness Kung-Fu has given her the advantage over every person every time. She can see that everyone needs help. Everyone seeks care, comfort, and love. The way that presents for different people will always be as diverse as the people themselves. And in so doing, Evelynt learns the true power of CVI: connection. Connectivity and multiversality are part of the same Circuit for a reason. The only way to truly understand how deeply connected everything in our universe is is to know that we are never really separate from one another. We always influence each other no matter what, whether directly or indirectly, locally or non-locally. Further, we exist in Many Worlds all at once. We are multifaceted, complex human beings. There are many parts of us strewn throughout many places, most of which we can’t physically reach. When we seek out new abilities and new ways of living, starting that journey can be difficult. Why not think that somewhere in another ‘verse there’s a version of you that’s already mastered a skill, completed a task, or become someone totally different?

Though Evelynt has mastered ‘Verse Jumping and has begun to master Kindness Kung-Fu, Jobu Tupaki still has an advantage over her mother. The power that comes from pain is often stronger than the power that comes from love. Where love takes work, pain doesn’t take effort to practice. Pain only takes feeling and allowing it in. The emotional pain that Jobu:Joy feels comes from a place where she doesn’t feel seen or understood by her family but especially from Evelyn no matter what ‘verse Jobu:Joy is occupying. That pain rests deep inside of a person and takes root. It gives Jobu the advantage in that her power, if never checked or questioned, is potentially limitless. The power of love, though strong, takes both internal and external effort to make it visible in ourselves:our Worlds. Our power to practice love can be limited by our available energy, our commitment to love, our need to show others and ourselves love based on our other feelings on any given day. Both pain and love can be equal in power but with Jobu, her advantage lies in her creative abilities. Jobu can change form at will bringing one version of herself from another ‘verse into the one she’s in. She created the Everything Bagel, an unprecedented source of pain and hatred that is so strong it threatens to destroy the multiverse. Creativity is power. It’s a power greater than people realize. By making things, we create the World we wish to occupy most. We practice love by putting love into our creations. We share love by sharing our creativity. And we make the Worlds better by being vulnerable enough to share what we love with others who may not like our works or judge us for them. The reactions of others isn’t important. What is important is creating for its own sake. It opens us up to new realities:’verse’s that make ourselves and those around us better. Creativity has the potential to save us all.
