# LMEEAAO
An Experiment in Multiversal Non-Fiction

On March 22, 2023, i acted heroically in a way few people ever do. i had an opportunity to save someone's life.

From that day on my reality shifted, altered, expanded, and created an awareness in me i had no prior conscious awareness of.
Soon after that i understood i had a greater part to play in my own life. i knew there was more for me to do than what i was used to. 
What i didn't expect was how far it would push me. What i didn't know was anything. What i thought i knew was everything.

It's common for people to, after long periods of emotional and physical stress, experiment with new ways of living. 
My new life journey was defined by spirituality. i had little background in understanding life beyond my senses though even those had been dulled in a spell of dissociation and possibly CPTSD for 36 years.  

In the 3 years following my moment of heroism i've been writing and refining a manuscript of my findings. i've built a cosmology, an understanding of our universe and my own multiverse.

By no means am i asserting that what i've written is scientific fact. i have no ability to experiment in "Western" scientific method to back up what i've claimed in this manuscript.
In fact, i readily admit the possibility that any truth in my work, as it exists in this moment, only applies to me.

_But that's only because no one else has seen it._

Only now as my life inches closer to 40 am i feeling the full breadth of my being. And as i get closer to that milestone i feel new shifts coming into my awareness.
i once thought this manuscript was meant to be a stand-alone book. i have edited it to be a philosophical Choose-Your-Own-Adventure. 
But time continues to move. My life continues to shift. And the real possibilities of what "America" is becoming are forcing my hand.

That force has inspired me. It gave me a chance to try something bold.

i realized the manuscript was never mine to begin with.
This work belongs to everyone.
i am only a conduit.
_**And i want you to refine it to be whatever you need it to be**_, which has been my intention with it from the start.

**Life, the Multiverse, and Everything Everywhere All At Once** or **LMEEAAO** is an opportunity to build not just a new world or new universe, but to shift our mindsets to expand to a mutliversal way of being.

i am not here to be your guide. i am not interested in telling you how i live so that you copy me. i can guarantee that what i experience is unique to me and if you tried to emulate it exactly you'd be frustrated. You'd think i'm a liar.
Instead, _i am here to tell my truth so that you can **prove** i'm a liar._

**LMEEAAO is an open-source cosmology. i am here to provide the initial framework of an understanding of reality i've purpose-built for myself.**

Where you come in is to take what i've built and tear it apart limb from limb.
_i want you, the reader, to become an active part of creating a new cosmology for yourself._ 

i encourage you actively edit the cosmology to question it, take what works, delete what doesn't, critique, edit, refine, experiment, get existential, cause yourself some confusion, share your commit with others, and work together to create cosmologies which resonate for and with you.

Some encouragements and guidance from me about this experiment (which i also encourage you to take or leave): We are not cult leaders. We are practicing taking the lead in our own lives. 
We are not sharing our ideas to force them on others. We are sharing ideas to react emergently to reality and create guidance that can continuously be refined so that we cannot be defined.
We understand that change is everything and is always available if not inevitable. We act not in reaction to life but in flow with it.

The main sections of the cosmology are as follows:

_**A Convergence of Many Worlds**_- An introduction to me and how i found this new life path. i explain how i connected to the film _Everything Everywhere All At Once_ and the book _Prometheus Rising_ which serves as a blueprint for the cosmology.

_**The Lower Circuits**_ and _**The Higher Circuits**_- i explain and analyze _EEAAO_ and my life using the framework of _Prometheus Rising_'s "Eight Circuits of Consciousness" model of reality. In general there are 3 sections per Circuit: An introduction to the Circuit, how my life corresponded to the Circuit, and an analysis of _EEAAO_ through the lens of the Circuit. There are 4 circuits total in _Lower_ and 4 in _Higher_. **Each Circuit will include questions to ask yourself so that you replace my life story and commit your own version of the work with your life story.**__

_**Beyond Circuit I-VIII**_ - i take influences from other media i've grown with and loved then show how i connect my cosmology to them. As of this writing i have entries for Circuits III, V, VI, VII, VIII.
