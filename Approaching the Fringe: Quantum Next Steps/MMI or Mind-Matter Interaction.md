With so many modalities available to me within this system i’ve built, i have found many ways to use them. The most dominant of alternative ways i experience my system is through Mind-Matter Interaction. My choice of term is specific. In the past there were ideas such as psychokinesis where people claim to use the power of their mind to directly affect objects within their local area. People with psychokinesis claim they must concentrate deeply and focus in order to manipulate their environment. Mind-Matter Interaction, for me, has NOTHING to do with focus or concentration. 

The way i experience my environment is reflective of the way i stay present. My ability to connect with people, animals, plant life, the wind, sun, and air are all dictated by how present i am. The connection i feel comes from my ability to understand that i move through life on multiple levels and tied into multiple systems. Not only do i occupy physical reality and experience classical physics as a result but simultaneously i occupy quantum reality and experience quantum physics in a tangible way.

The interconnection of all things has many names and ideas to go along with it: Animism, Gaia Hypothesis, Anima Mundi, Tao Te Ching…Time and again esoteric thought:spirituality has returned to this idea that all things affect and are affected by one another. Many ideas have gone as far as suggesting that there is no space between objects or living things; the only separation between objects comes from the belief of an observer that objects are separate. Modern “Western” science is finally coming to an understanding of reality that corroborates this. A large swath of “Western” science has moved beyond the reductivist leanings of its previous generations and is embracing complexity: the places where individual systems overlap and share information. More and more individual researchers or teams of scientists are finding themselves hitting dead ends in their studies because they will suddenly be faced with a problem that can only be answered with an understanding of a different system. 

Using my understandings of quantum mechanics, synchronicity, interconnection, and my framework for quantum being the Many Worlds theory, i’ve found i do have MMI abilities. But how does that work if i don’t concentrate or focus on my environment the way people with psychokinesis do? i base my experience of reality on all the aforementioned modalities with one caveat: i simultaneously believe and do not believe in any of them. Think of it this way: MMI, as i experience it, is a reflection of the state of my presence of self and my observation of reality. Reality changes as i move through it, observe it, and interact with it. My sense of occupying physical space is informed by both classical and quantum physics. As a result, some of the actions i take affect quantum reality. Principles of quantum mechanics such as superposition and entanglement are touchstones of my experience. Just as the orbit, spin, or velocity of a particle entangled with an atom cannot be known until it is measured, i cannot know when, where, or how MMI will affect my reality until i observe and measure it through my interpretation of an MMI event. The trick to affecting quantum reality through MMI is my simultaneous belief and disbelief. This creates a space within my mind:understanding that acts as though it were superposition. i get to experience my locality with an interpretation that acts as though it were entanglement showing its true nature. Just like we can act as though the brain is a computer, i can do the same thing for how i interpret my reality.

The most common events i interpret as MMI are “pop” noises in local space. Things like house and/or object settling noises, glitches in electronics, and inexplicable feelings can all be interpreted by me as MMI. The impetus for MMI is usually a private thought in my mind. For example, i may be deep in thought trying to make a choice between multiple actions to take. Maybe i’ve been invited to a kickback on one side of town or to a date on the other. Sitting in concentration and thinking about what to do, an MMI event occurs. The most recent thought i had before the MMI occurred will then by interpreted by me as the best choice to make. i experience glitches in electronics in a different way: my current emotional state has consistently been the best way to gauge why some gadget i’m trying to use is refusing to cooperate. The most common emotional reasons behind an electronic glitch for me are powerful in their own right: excitement, anger, sadness. In whatever way i’m emotionally expressive, this changes the way my electronics respond to me. In order for me to resolve the issue i will do a brief Circuit Check and, if necessary, return to homeostasis then move at least within Circuit IV. After doing that, i have found my electronics tend to resume their intended functions.

Just as with issues of self-control, presence, and meditation, forgetting about MMI then remembering it exists is a practice. If i keep thinking about MMI, i will not have experiences of it. If i forget MMI exists for a time, i may have an experience of it. The nebulous space created by forgetting:remembering and belief:disbelief is itself upheld by a balancing act. The more i remember, the more i might believe and believing means i will not have MMI experience. It is better to forget and not believe in it so that when an MMI experience does happen, i will notice it and believe in my interpretation of it.

All of these functions are completely made up by me and as such hold only as much power over me as i decide they do. i will admit that my life has become much more enjoyable, easier to navigate, and fulfilling having used these functions as much as i have. However, it is important to me to keep a healthy distance from my own belief system as much as possible. The last thing i want is to succumb to spiritual psychosis. My reality must remain grounded for me to be present and interact with others. i must be able to notice the distinct difference between my beliefs and my own world. As i move through life i continue to keep my heart and mind open to all possibilities. For me, that’s enough. Not every moment must be dictated through the lens of my personal system of understanding.
