_Questions for Self-Assessment_

1. Can you briefly sum up what the first ten years of your life were like? What were the major touchstones of your life?

2. What were your family dynamics like? Did you have parents:caretakers:guardians that you spent more time with or who paid more attention to you than others? If you did not have these figures in your life, who did you learn to look up to and let influence you?

3. What was something that you loved to do as a kid that you don't do anymore? Would you do it again if you could? Why or why not?
