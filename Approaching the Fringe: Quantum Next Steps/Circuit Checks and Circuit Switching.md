Once i taught myself how to complete an entire circuit of meditation using the Circuits as a guide, i realized there was more i could do with the Eight Circuits as a complete system. Using these individual modifications of the meditation, i’m able to hone in on any distress i may be feeling and help myself feel them in totality then let them go. i can also catch myself in moments of awareness to see which Circuit i’m currently operating in and assess if i need to be in a higher or lower Circuit depending on the situation i’m in.

A Circuit Check is what i’ve come to call my ability to determine where in the Circuits my mind is most likely operating. This technique amounts to a truncated version of a full meditation circuit. Using the knowledge i’ve accrued about each Circuit’s functions, purpose(s), and physical locations, i perform a Circuit Check step by step from CI to CVIII. What’s important to note is that in times of extreme distress, the ability to reach certain circuits can be locked out. For example, if i was in a terrifying situation that demanded i take immediate action to save my life i would be locked into CI until i resolved that issue. Only after my body is safe and cared for could i move into higher Circuits. 

Circuit Checks are useful in times of distress that aren’t linked to CI. Anywhere from CII upward is where this tool is best utilized. Once i find which Circuit i’m operating from, i can take that information and troubleshoot. Have i just gotten out of an argument with someone and am feeling emotional after it’s over? i’m likely in CII. Have i been lost in daydreams and need to come back to the present? That’s CIII. And so on. Each Circuit has its own remedies but the first step is always to become aware enough of the state of my mind so that i might help myself approach homeostasis. Some ways to stabilize each Circuit may include:

CI - Resolving whatever issue is an immediate threat by taking necessary steps. This is the realm of fight:flight:freeze:fawn.

CII - Recognizing that i am in emotional distress, identifying the emotion both by physical location in the body and by name in my mind, recognizing i am the observer of my emotions and not the emotions themselves to give myself space from them, and then letting the feelings pass as naturally as possible. i do not try to hold on to the emotion to tightly or try to shove it away from me as neither results in homeostasis but instead greater bodily distress and a regular drip feed of adrenaline.

CIII - Become aware of the direction of time i find myself in. If i find my mind is in the past, i do what i can to recognize and feel through any regrets, sense of shame, or missed opportunities i may have realized in a way similar to CII. If i’m feeling anxious, unsure, or worried, i tell myself that future outcomes are not already determined and set myself at ease about whatever situation i’m imagining. No matter the direction of time, using grounding techniques after moving through my feelings brings me back to the present and allows me to practice my time binding techniques and rationality.

CIV - If i have been in a socially challenging situation where my “morality”, social status, or sexuality have been challenged i feel through them, again like CII. After that, i take time to reaffirm who i am to myself. i remember that i am not defined by the observations or assessments of others and i never will be. Only i can define my self. Often i will do breath work to bring my awareness into focus and soothe my self.

CV - If the issue is spiritual or religious, i recognize that no one way of understanding life is more or less “correct” or “true” than any others. The ultimate truth of spirit, in my understanding, is the creation of systems that enhance the life of the practitioner and does not cause harm to others. As long as i remain aligned to those ideals, i find i can ease myself out of CV distress well. If the issue is more focused on wholeness of self i might take calming actions for my body. There are certain places on my body i might rub to enhance feeling such as the back of my neck where the vagus nerve is or my forearms which have always been a particularly sensitive area for me. i might also take a bath, eat something i like, or masturbate. Any or all of these in combination can enhance my return to self or, if done in excess, could become unhealthy coping mechanisms. i do my best to stay mindful of this risk.

CVI - These stresses are some of the most difficult to deal with. The knowledge of suffering that we who have access to the internet understand and encounter on a daily basis only continues to rise as our systems fail. The best salve for these wounds are practices of creativity, hope, and joy. Every day i find reasons to continue doing my work and cultivate the drive to create, the hope that life can always get better, and find small, joyous moments that enhance the previous two. i have no space for long-term despair. i do have the capacity to care for others and take action for all of us. That’s what i focus on.

CVII - If i find myself trying to force a change in my programming, i stop to assess any underlying issues. Why do i want to change this aspect of myself so badly that i’m causing myself distress? What is the goal of my reprogramming this aspect? What steps, if any, can i take in physical space to make this task easier? These questions and others may lead to a brainstorming session or even dropping the reprogramming task for the time being. i know i can always return, most likely with a fresh perspective, at another time.

CVIII - Feeling non-local distress is an indication that someone or something i’m connected to is in distress. These are the moments where a sudden, inexplicable fear or doubt seem to wash over me with no discernible cause in the space around me. Often i can get messaging from another version of me:the collective consciousness:my intuition and know who to reach out to. If that inclination isn’t correct, i may try a few more people. If no one i reach is the one i felt for, i let it rest and leave my self open to further communication from sources internal or external.

By the time i’ve completed as many Checks as i need to, i will either have returned my self to homeostasis or be well on my way towards it. As with any system that attempts to exert control over chaos, the mind:brain:body connection does not accept being forced to comply with orders. My attempt to calm myself is an invitation to experience in full whatever is coming to me, accept that my system needs to feel these things, and then ease myself into a state where adrenaline is not actively being created and fed to me internally. Where before i was completely without recourse when i was overwhelmed, this series of action i can take now gives me not only a means by which to self soothe but also a sense that i am working with the totality of my system not only on multiple scales but in multiple interconnected ways.

Circuit Switching is another variation on how i utilize the Circuits. No matter what, my mind is always operating within the confines of at least one Circuit (The only exception to this is CVII which can utilize multiple Circuits in an attempt to meta-program). Life can surprise me in many ways. The situations i put my self into may challenge me and make me feel unable to perform in ways i’d like. Maybe i’m just coming out of a Circuit Check and want to move my mind into another Circuit so that i might relax or pay more attention to someone or something. The reason to Switch can vary but the intent is always the same: be in a mental state which differs from the Circuit i currently reside.

To make a Switch, i first have to investigate the current state of my mind and determine which Circuit i’m in. Using the same techniques as Meditation and Check, i ask my self questions, investigate my body and my physical state, then make my determination. After i’ve decided which Circuit my mind is, i decide where i need it to be instead. Am i coming out of an emotionally distressing situation but need to perform in service of capital ASAP? The route for that is to go from CII to CIV. This presents a difficulty: in-between those two is CIII. In order for me to successfully make the switch, i MUST move through CIII to reach CIV. The only way out of CII is through. Just like in a Check, i determine when my mind is (either past or future) and find my way back to the present with grounding techniques, deep listening, and increased awareness. Of all the ways to switch, i’ve found the move between CV and CVI the easiest. Otherwise, the chances of leaving one Circuit for another will likely involve some form of self-assessment:self care.

In theory, i should be able to perform this task anywhere, anytime. In practice, i struggle to Switch “live” (in the sense that i am actively in a social situation, either in private or public, and want:need to Switch for a better life experience). It’s not often that i find myself struggling to engage socially these days but every rule has exceptions. Most commonly i begin to flounder socially in the presence of people i admire or am intimidated by. This is sadly one of my greatest weaknesses: the people i would like to connect with the most are always the hardest for me to engage with in a natural way. i’ve lightly attempted to investigate the root cause of this several times. Is this social anxiety a matter of chemistry? Is it my expectations versus my reality? Do those i admire and my current self engage in more than one timeline and there’s an unknown friction causing my self:selves to react? The variables are too numerous to list. Whatever the case(s), i continue to struggle in this way and have yet to find a working solution when i encounter it. Using sheer force of will to push myself towards making the connection i seek has been the go-to response for now. It doesn’t always work, but i would much rather take chances than be left always wondering “What if”?
