CI is survival instinct, pure and simple. Whatever it takes to keep oneself alive against the elements, animals, and all dangers is its function. We move towards safety and move away from harm, making use of whatever skills or advantages we may have against whatever threatens us.

CII brings emotions and territoriality to the front of our consciousness. Developing an emotional understanding of oneself and others allows tribal:familial functions to take hold. We learn how to act amongst the group of people we find ourselves surrounded by. We may follow, bend, or break the rules we’re presented with. These actions have consequences.

CIII informs us of our place in time. Depending on how we focus our attention, we can change where we “exist” in either the past, present, or future. Our ability to remember the past can harm us if we allow it to be all that we focus on. Our ability to envision the future can get us stuck wishing and hoping for things to be different without taking action. Rationality can aid in remaining present but it can also hinder the mind by becoming too focused on “what is” and rarely, if ever, allowing space for “what could be” unless backed up by substantial empirical evidence.

CIV ensures that you take on the lifestyle of the people nearest you, your tribe or family, by adopting a code of ethics and morality. Should you break these rules and challenge the status quo, you’ll be seen as an outsider and marginalized. Your way of living, the way you decide is best for moving through the world, is a threat to anyone who doesn’t live the same way. Mainly, morality is tied up in social standards and sexuality. Your public-facing life is supposed to conform to the norms, and your personal life is where you practice what you really want. This naturally causes shame and self-loathing if what’s practiced in private challenges your morality or the morality of your tribe:family.

CV adds a spiritual or religious aspect to one’s overall understanding of life and one’s place in it. Oftentimes this Circuit’s functions are conflated with baggage from CII and CIV, specifically territory and morality. However, should one step outside of the expectations of their tribe:family, they may find new rules, morals, and ways of being that challenge those aforementioned Circuits. Whatever the case, the function of CV is to allow a person the emotional space to understand that they are but a small part of existence and that something bigger than them (whatever it is or may be) humbles them and keeps their understanding of life grounded in the physical while allowing for exploration in mental space and beyond it. Often there are even more rules that apply to someone who practices spirituality or religion which keeps the user reminded of their simultaneous place both here and elsewhere. This leads to a sense of holism, of being holistic. You are the sum total of all your parts and understandings.

You are greater than the sum of your individual parts. You are gestalt. ∞ What is Bell’s Theorem? RAW does a fair job of making that work for his purposes but we need to go deeper for mine. First, let’s look at Bell’s Inequality which states that two particles when created during a single process are entangled and remain that way even over vast distances. Entanglement can be shown in experiments by affecting one aspect of one of the entangled particles (such as the particle’s spin, as every particle has a unique spin that depends on how the particle is interacted with) and measuring the other entangled particle. To start, both particles are considered to be in superposition, or that both particles exist in a probability of multiple states at the same time. The experiment being done to one particle, like measuring its spin, shows researchers time and again that the particle not being directly affected by measurement will still reflect the same result as its entangled particle equal. That is, what happens to one particle happens to the other instantaneously without violating special relativity or the speed of light. This communication is debated as to whether it is a sharing of information. From a technical standpoint, the change from the experimented-upon particle being reflected by the resulting change in the entangled particle cannot be an exchange of information. If it was information, by the rulesets of everything humanity knows about energy in relation to physics, this change would necessarily need to violate the speed of light. Energy cannot violate the speed of light.

∞  Is information the same as data? No. Think of it this way: data is a collection of figures that comprise facts. Data can consist of large amounts of facts. For example, let's say you gathered the ages of every person who lives in an apartment complex. That set of numbers is data because it is a large amount of factual figures that represent the total of all the resident’s ages. For that data to become information, there needs to be study and interpretation of the data that then makes that data into information. Now that you have the data that comprises all the ages of the residents of the apartment complex, what you do with the data will become information. How many of the residents are age 50 or older? Once you’ve figured that out, that’s information. Information gives data context and meaning. So when one entangled particle is measured, it exchanges data with its paired particle to reflect the same state in each particle at the same time. Without the observer who is measuring the first particle, the observer who is monitoring the second particle would not be able to see the exchange of data between particles and then interpret that exchange, which makes that data into information.

∞ This then leads us into the ideas of locality and non-locality. Locality, also known as local reality, is defined as a quantum particle only being able to affect another physically nearby (local) particle while the properties of both particles have real values that can be measured and recorded (such as spin). Before the discovery of quantum physics, classical physics asserted that all things would need to be local in order for them to affect each other. An apple falling and hitting Sir Isaac Newton on the head is an example of locality. The apple exists in physical space, hanging above Newton who is physically nearby the apple. When the apple falls and hits his head, that entire exchange has happened locally. As for non-locality, i’ve already given you the perfect example: entanglement. When one of two particles is measured, and those particles are nowhere near one another in physical (local) space, both particles then reflecting the same measurable data proves that there are ways particles can affect one another in non-local space. The physical components, the particles, must exist in physical space which makes them local. If that’s the case, then the data is non-local since it must exist somewhere, somewhen, that isn’t in any one place.

The Whole System, then, comprises every known part of the entirety of our universe. Every star, planet, nebula, asteroid, black hole, galaxy, and so on are part of The Whole System. Our planet (holistic) is one of many planets that then make up our solar system (gestalt). Our solar system (holistic) is part of a group of solar systems that make up our galaxy (gestalt). Our galaxy (holistic) is part of a large cluster of galaxies that then make up our local group (gestalt). This “pulling back” of a single viewpoint to see larger and larger parts of existence that comprise our universe proves that we are only as important as the scale by which we measure importance.
