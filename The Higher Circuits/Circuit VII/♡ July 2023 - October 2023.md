My state of neurosomatic bliss was fully integrated into my mind:brain by this time. My experience of the world, in terms of physical perception, seemed to oppose the feelings that were dominating my mind. Though it was sharp and ready to take in information in new:novel ways, my brain had added a soft, hazy filter over my visual experience. It was as if i was looking at the world through a semi-transparent screen of finely woven fabric. The world and everything in it seemed to radiate a glow around the edges of objects. When i concentrated directly onto something or someone, my perception would sharpen and leave the background to be hazy and out of focus. It was also around this time that i realized part of my issue with electronic devices with screens was not just my brain rejecting them totally but my eyes becoming significantly more sensitive to light sources of all kinds. In order to protect my eyes, i began using sunglasses as my daily wear visual aid. Not only did i notice an immediate relief to the brightness of my world, but an exciting secondary effect came of it. The lenses of my sunglasses are a semi-transparent, rusty orange color which still allows people to see my eyes. This is great for building connection and awareness, sure. But what it offered as a bonus was that people had a difficult time gendering me properly. The added layer of obfuscation gave me an advantage i had long craved but didn’t know how to capture: i now appeared outside as genderless as i felt inside. My body dysmorphia and gender dysphoria both decreased significantly. i was finally in a place where i felt i could be perceived by others in a way that would cause them confusion on how to approach and speak to me. It gave me the advantage of being able to approach others with more ease and purpose.

Since i was feeling more easy and present in my body than ever before, my awareness was more accessible than ever. There was an ever-present sense of calm in my life. My actions were assured, ideas came to me quickly with a clarity of execution i’d never experienced before. What was shocking was how people around me seemed to detect or otherwise understand just how collected and present i was. i’m not talking about the people i choose to keep in my life but rather total strangers. People would approach me, open up to me, and want to connect with me. i didn’t feel as though i was holding myself in a way that invited more connection or exuded any increased confidence but others saw something in me. There were more times than i could count that i was offered small kindnesses for no reason. For instance, i visited a branded ice cream shop i’d never been to before some time in August 2023. The flavors on offer were uncommon, combining fresh fruits and sometimes even cheeses to their blends. My interest was piqued but i wasn’t sure what to try. Once it was my turn at the counter, i asked the person serving me what they preferred, mentioning it was my first time there. After some recommendations and a little small talk, the person decided they would pay for my ice cream cone. i was surprised! i struggled to understand why they would do that for me so i asked if they were sure. They said not to worry about it, got my cone, and let me walk out the door without a cent exchanged. This wasn’t the only time i experienced such kindness during this time frame but the stories are all essentially the same: i showed other people the kindness and attention i felt capable of giving, and others responded to it by showing me, a total stranger, a gesture of thanks. The connectivity i was feeling could have only been the results of my state of bliss and the regulation of my nervous system at their combined peak.

Also within this period of my heightened state of being i found my paramour Amy and myself experiencing a kind of connection that reached beyond our physical proximities and into places i hadn’t previously known. i’ve seen people talk about how deeply they understand their partners, how they know them so well that they start to finish each other’s sentences, predict their needs, or seem to communicate without needing to physically speak to one another through any physical medium. How many of those same people would be able to speak to this sense of interconnectivity without having to be near their sweetheart? What about being 30 miles away, feeling the other person think about them, and immediately sending a text while the other person sends a text at the same time? The number of times Amy and i communicated like this was so regular it was the expected norm after a while. It was surprising every time. Our communication shared a kind of magic that i can only surmise came from invisible forces (more to come on this later in Circuit VIII).

This was around the same time i started my new job selling skate shoes. i had placed myself into a situation where the expectation was to speak with customers regularly, building earnest connections and making sure they were satisfied with their purchases. i was coming from a place where only months ago the idea of having to sell anything would have made me nauseous. During the peak of my neurosomatic bliss, though? i was excited at the prospect. All i wanted to do was talk to other people about anything and everything that came up. i felt compelled to practice speaking with as many people as i could each day. The joy i was feeling from just the idea of trying to make friends with customers was palpable. It didn’t take me long to get the hang of the job. Before i knew it, i was performing nearly as well as some of the long-time employees and was proving myself to be a smart choice of hire. 

What was difficult for me however was trying to remain calm and focused on connecting with my fellow employees. The forces i had been reborn with seemed to be pushing me to share my experience and knowledge with anyone who would listen. It was difficult to abstain from spouting off every little spiritual:esoteric thought that sprang from my mind. i was falling prey to one of the most common follies of the newly Awakened: trying to get everyone to understand:think like me because i felt it was the only way i could be understood. 

My coworkers came to expect my strangeness quickly after i was hired. i wasn’t exactly trying to hide it. By this time i had a few pieces of jewelry that i wore daily and continued to wear while on the clock that represented my beliefs. Since my belief system was highly personalized, i got away with wearing what, on the outside, seemed like benign trinkets. To me, these items kept me more deeply connected to what i was feeling and how i existed. Both of the items i kept on my left hand and wrist respectively: a small, plastic google eye ring with two moving eyes and a bracelet my paramour Amy bought me of a hamsa, the hand with an evil eye on the palm. Hamsa are a recognizable spiritual symbol to some. They represent protection, most specifically divine protection, with the word hamsa translating to “five” from Arabic. Though the name itself has a specific cultural connection, the symbol has also been known as part of Jewish traditions as well. Having grown up in a culturally Jewish household, i was drawn to the hamsa as a way to keep that connection close. The fact that it contained an eye was a bonus to my understanding considering the google eyes that are pervasive throughout EEAAO.

Regardless, i was asked by my coworkers about my jewelry a few times. i was also asked about why i wore sunglasses. While it was true that the sunglasses greatly helped with my light sensitivity issues, i never mentioned their alternative purpose of obfuscation. Connecting with my coworkers was never strained but rarely was it easy. i inhabited this space between ages that made meaningful conversations difficult. Most of my coworkers were in their early 20’s at most. i wasn’t very familiar with their choices of music, the visual media they consumed, and so much more. The age gap between management and most of the employees was in my favor since management were in their 30’s like i was. Most of the time i stayed back and let people discuss what they would and would find places to pop in as i saw fit. But if anyone gave me the time to talk about what was going on in my life or what was on my mind, the Awakened stream of consciousness flowed forth without anything to stop it aside my ability to read the comfort (or discomfort) of the person or people i was talking to. This problem was largely between my coworkers and myself. i never approached esoteric territory with customers unless they mentioned something first (most of the time it was a comment about my google eye ring or some of the enamel pins on my lanyard). Around my coworkers it was harder to control this urge.

i’m happy to say that despite these strained, sometimes odd exchanges i never (as far as i know) made anyone feel uneasy around me. My presence in the store gave rise to interesting conversations that might not have happened otherwise, which was fun. Overall i think my mind being so ready to connect:make connections gave me an edge in meeting sales goals and completing daily tasks but i struggled to remain “down to earth” enough to make myself feel at ease. That sense of ease never truly came to me. Not long after starting this job, my mind began to loosen its grip on this near-constant sense of bliss. Where once i was constantly happy, able to communicate easily, and find connections between disparate concepts with little effort, now my mind was beginning to get used to the new connections it had made. What were once new pathways throughout the physical parts of my brain were becoming well-worn creases. In short, the novelty of my experience was beginning to wear off.

Novelty, or novel information, is understood to be information that is new or unprecedented to those that find it. My experience of the world as a whole at this time was novel: i had never known anything about quantum theory, metaphysics, spirituality, practices of attention, or any esoteric works before my Awakening. After plunging myself into all of these new ways of viewing the world head (or more accurately, brain) first, they began to feel familiar. Familiarity is what makes the novel rote. Understanding new concepts necessarily makes those concepts no longer seem as exciting as when first found. This was something that i started to struggle with as soon as i recognized it was happening.

i continue to repeat this point because it carries a lot of emotional weight for me and i feel it helps me be better understood but, again, having NEVER been a person of religious background or spiritual understanding, i found myself becoming depressed and full of self-loathing over this gradual loss of my neurosomatic bliss. More than those feelings though, was an encroaching sense of fear: fear that my mind was losing its ability to understand the world in this new, exciting way, fear that i was no longer going to be “special” or “unique”, fear that i was going to lose all the wonderful new people in my life. At the time i didn’t understand just how much of this fear was tied to ego. The words “special” and “unique” should have been a give away but i was too wrapped up in scarcity to be able to see myself as clearly as i could have. The combination of all these factors (depression, self-loathing, fear) made my mind a battleground once more. Where before my Awakening i never had a moment's rest from a mind always in motion with thoughts and problem solving, now my mind was fighting for peace, calm, and presence in the moment. Part of me was trying to force the novel feelings to the forefront of my experience while the rest was encouraging me to let myself be. 

Some days felt like a struggle to stay alive in my Awakened self. i could feel a sense of my old mindset creeping around in the proverbial shadows of my mind. There were feelings of being far removed from my body, from reality, and from the people in my life. There were times i tried to ignore it. Those were the times that made me feel embattled with my self. Ignoring the sense of loss:change that was happening to me only made those underlying feelings hurt worse. i never tried to outright embrace the loss of novelty i was experiencing. i tried to make the best of the situation. i found myself doubling down on talking about my experiences to others. i poured my self into my work and my writing. i read extensively, making notes and highlights throughout them. i found myself becoming more concerned with the everyday issues i had left behind for months: money, food, shelter… The reality tunnel i had built for my self was beginning to crack. The way i moved through the world was showing its incompatibility with consensus reality. Other than trying to distract myself with dating or information, what i mainly did was go out and spend money i didn’t have on things i didn’t need or rest deeply. i bought more movies on physical media in this time than i had inclination to make use of them and watch them. The rest i was getting was focused on my integration of the Eight Circuits into my mindset and discovering how to further manipulate my brain to work in new ways. It was at this time that i started to more fully understand what it meant for me to meta-program myself.

Without the understanding of meta-programming i laid out in the previous section, i couldn’t make sense of what its functions as a modality was. As i spent more and more time in states of meditation and rest, i found myself more and more able to be an observer of my mind and its functions instead of a passive participant in my machinations. Only after many months of focusing on being the observer of my thoughts, detaching my self from the ideas in my head being “me” or “who i am” did i reach toward the separation from self that meta-programming demands. Even my Awakening itself only moved me from one system of understanding to another without this loss of individualism that our shared System embeds within us. All i had done was swap one system for another and become fully engrossed in it. My need to share my experience was wrapped up in a self-imposed dogma, a set of rules meant to force my belief system into me. i wasn’t able to see just how adherent i had become to the rules for living i had made up for myself. That idea, that i was making up the rules as i went, finally sank into my understanding far enough to allow me the space to become an observer of self no longer bound to what i had prescribed for my mind and experiences. 

Even having gained that extra space between the egoic me that had emerged from my Awakening experience and the me that was observing it, i continued to push myself and my experience toward the Circuits as often as i could remember to. Something i continue to struggle with to this day is memory. My memory has been by affected by my traumas, my mental illnesses, health & genetics, our shared System, and so much more. This isn’t unique, of course. So many people can say the same things, maybe more. Why i bring up my memory now is my understanding of spirituality has informed my need for ritual. For whatever reason(s?), it’s frighteningly easy for me to lose my spiritual framework when i’m not mindful enough of it. Rituals help bring my awareness back to the rules i’ve built for myself. During this “waning period” of my Awakening, as the novelty wore off my focus on ritual took hold. i spent time meditating, of course, but also listening to certain music ⚓, reading certain books, focusing on phenomena i recognized as synchronicity ⛛, and raising my spatial awareness to look for signs and signifiers that recalled the Circuits (my personal practice around numerology for one).

To keep myself engaged at work, i’d lean into these rituals. My capacity for social engagement has always been fairly low and at such a social job, the need to retain much of that energy was important. It wasn’t just my social energy that was affected but my physical energy, too. Moving at pace between the stockroom in the back and the store itself was more movement than i was used to at the time. Juggling all of these different systems of energy and ritual each day i worked helped me retain the mindset i wanted. Otherwise i would find myself chasing other highs like shopping, food, and electronics in my time off. Another part of my grounding techniques was going to a local cafe to work on my own personal work. The beginnings of this book were written there and many books read there. i also developed a report with some of the employees and a few of the locals, too. Not everyone was comfortable with my presence there but that’s the beauty of my existence while there: i was queering the space internally and externally through making art and engagement with the people there.

Turning my life into one long chain of rituals wasn’t just relegated to work. At the aforementioned cafe, any store i would go to, any place at all was a playground for me 

to practice the ways i understood and moved through the world. i struggled to accept that i was getting checked out by people (and when i say people i mean from younger to older with no boundaries around gender that i noticed).
