From the ground up, i’ve built an understanding of the world that refuses to be treated like any other. Does this new system of understanding, this ontology, force strict rulesets on me? No. Multiversality, the name i’ve given my ontology, has given me guidance. That guidance is something i can choose to accept or ignore. There’s no need for me to force myself into a specific way of thinking (there’s no dogma) because there is no one to answer to besides who i am at any given time. Multiversality is adaptable: i can take in new information from different sources, figure out if that information feels correct, and add it to my understanding. Multiversality is ephemeral: it doesn’t occupy my mind at all times as the only way it really works is if i don’t believe in it completely. Does that mean my ontology even works at all? i built it and even i can’t say for sure.

The reason i built my understanding of the world is because there were no other religions, spiritualities, or systems that resonated with me. From a young age i was introduced to Judaism and Catholicism which i rejected. Perhaps it was because i saw the ways the adults in my life acted, and their actions told me there was nothing worth believing in for me when it came to those religions. As i grew into a pre-teen and a teenager, i spent time in school libraries where i read about Islam, Buddhism, Taoism, alternative spiritualities, occultism…honestly any system for understanding the world that was available eventually hit my hands and was read about. When i had unrestricted internet access, part of what i browsed for were different religions. Most of what i found were cynical, sarcastic, or tongue in cheek. Dudeism based on The Dude from The Big Lebowski, the Flying Spaghetti Monster taking the piss out of all belief systems, even my most deeply enjoyed franchise as a teen, The Matrix, had a burgeoning dogma that i found no reason to take to heart.

As i’ve described throughout this book, what i needed was to be broken by life so that i could rebuild my world in my own image. Through processing my traumas, paying attention to the media i consumed, and piecing together disparate works, i created a mental playground that had everything in it i could want. Multiversality is still growing, still changing. i have no interest in staying the same person i am today. i’m not even the same person i was when i started to write this book. i have given myself a gift greater than i could have expected: a way to move through the world that’s uniquely my own and  feels good to practice and believe:not believe in.

Besides applying my reinterpretation of the Eight Circuits of Consciousness model to my life, what else can be done with Multiversality? Plenty! Over these next pages, i’ll describe to you how i use my version of the Eight Circuits in various ways. i’ve developed a meditation technique that utilizes the Circuits and can be practiced anywhere at any time. Using that technique as a baseline, i’ve also developed a way to feel into my body, my mind, and my self and engage with the feelings and emotions i find there. This has helped me find my way back to homeostasis, giving me access to a calm and regulated nervous system in times i need it most. Further, using specific Circuits i can prepare myself to experience the world in ways that enhance wonder, curiosity, and awe. Lastly, using all of these techniques i explain how i can manipulate physical space without physically interacting with it. When my own experiences meet with quantum reality, there can be surprising results.
