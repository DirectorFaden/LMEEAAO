_Exercises for Self-Assessment_

1. Can you name things about yourself you believe are true? What are they?

2. Of those beliefs, can you remember where/when/how/from who or what you learned them?

3. Do any of those beliefs limit or restrict you? What do they prevent/keep you safe from?

4. Do you believe in those beliefs? Can you pull your thinking further away from them and think about them from the outside?

5. What's something that you can do to prove any limiting beliefs aren't real? Do it now if you're able. If you can't do it now, make a step-by-step plan that will help you achieve your proof then act on it.
