_EXERCISES FOR SELF-ASSESSMENT_

1. What parts of this story, if any, resonate with your life experiences?

2. Have you ever had a moment where your life changed dramatically after it happened? What led you to that moment? What changed afterwards?

3. What kinds of media did you interact with before that moment (if any)? What media did you find after?

4. Go back and re-experience that media now. Look for themes in it. Are there any spiritual themes? If so, what are they?

5. How do you define spirituality? How do you define religion?
